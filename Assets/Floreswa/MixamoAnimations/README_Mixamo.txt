Put Mixamo FBX animation downloads in this folder.

Recommended starter files:
- Idle.fbx
- Walking.fbx
- Running.fbx

Mixamo download settings:
- Format: FBX for Unity
- Skin: Without Skin for animation-only downloads
- Frames Per Second: 30
- Keyframe Reduction: None

After adding files, run:
Mobil OFL > Characters > Build Floreswa Mixamo Animator
Mobil OFL > Production > Integrate Floreswa Characters
